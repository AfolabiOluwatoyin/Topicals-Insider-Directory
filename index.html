What the Insider Directory could see, once it's tagged

The new Insiders directory collects one open text field for content style. Lovely concept, questionable execution, once there are hundreds of responses and no way to search them. Below is a working example of turning those answers into a directory you can actually filter, using invented sample profiles, not real community members.

Today's form: one open answer

Tell us about your content style
A keyword search here only catches people who happened to use that exact word, someone who wrote "outfit content" instead of "fashion" gets missed entirely.

Tagged from that same answer

This response gets tagged "Fashion x Skincare Crossover" no matter which words it uses, so it surfaces under either category, not just the one exact word someone happened to type.
